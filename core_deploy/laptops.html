Deep Silicon Microarchitecture

Apple's Unified Approach: The M5 Max effectively scales the 2nm architecture to support a monstrous 800GB/s pipeline. Because the 40-core GPU and the 16-core CPU share the exact same physical package memory pool, there is literally zero copy-latency when transferring massive local AI models or rendering 8K optical timelines.

"The Neural Engine's 45 TOPS operates silently underneath system workloads, permitting full CoreML compilation without engaging the cooling fans."

Intel's x86 Revolution: Lunar Lake physically embeds up to 32GB of LPDDR5X directly onto the processor tile alongside the compute cores. By eliminating traditional SO-DIMM paths and entirely stripping out hyperthreading in favor of advanced Lion Cove P-cores, IPC single-thread performance drastically increases while energy expenditure hits an historic low.

"Acer's integration of the Arc Xe2 graphics enables high-level DirectX12 compute directly within an incredibly dense thermal execution envelope."
